What this is for

A throwaway rig for feel-testing three things before any Expo code gets written. Play for five minutes on your phone and judge only these:

1 · Tap-to-merge

Tapping a creature merges it with a matching neighbour. Compare it to dragging (both work). Does removing the drag make it meaningfully faster?

2 · The cascade

From tier 5, merges fire Surge — but only inside a ring that grows with tier (2 cells at tier 5, the whole board at tier 9). Targets light up gold before they move, and it grabs the closest pairs first, so the tidier the board, the bigger the cascade. From tier 7, Ignite also burns low-tier clutter inside that ring back into energy.

Surge always does something. With no pair in range it drags the nearest creature to you (Gathered) to set up your next merge; if it is hemmed in it refunds energy instead. The question: does the cascade read as a consequence of where you chose to merge?

3 · The chain meter

Each merge opens a 2.5s window. Only your own merges climb the multiplier — cascade merges pay coins and extend the window, but never raise it for you. A big cascade hands you time to keep playing rather than playing on your behalf. Multiplier affects coins only.

Deliberately unlike the real game

  • Energy regenerates ~40× faster so a session is testable
  • Abilities start at tier 5 — rarer than before, so they stay an event
  • One family stands in for all eight, so Surge and Ignite sit back to back
  • Placeholder tiles — tier as heat and a numeral, no art
  • Long-press any creature to bulk-merge every pair of that tier
  • Vibration works on Android; iOS Safari ignores it
